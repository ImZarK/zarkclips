Zark Clips – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Zark Clips is a personal tool used only by its owner (TikTok account @imzarckk) to publish his own gaming video clips to his own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

When the owner authorizes the app with TikTok Login Kit, the app receives basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to upload and publish videos to that account (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).

How data is used

This information is used only to publish the owner's own videos and to confirm which account is connected. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared, sold or sent to third parties.

Data retention and deletion

No data is stored on any server. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Apps and services), and the local tokens are then deleted.

Contact

For questions, contact the owner through TikTok: @imzarckk.
